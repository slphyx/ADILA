WHO AWaRe book definition

Severity Assessment and Considerations:

Children with pneumonia:

  • Should be treated with oral amoxicillin at home with home care advise
    1. Fast breathing (respiratory rate > 50 breaths/ minute in children aged 2-11 months; respiratory rate > 40 breaths/minutes in children aged 1-5 years)
    2. Chest indrawing

Children with sever pneumonia (or a child with pneumonia who cannot tolerate oral antibiotics):

  • Should be admitted to hospital and treated with intravenous antibiotics
  • Severe pneumonia is characterized by signs of pneumonia:
    • Fast breathing (+/- chest indrawing) PLUS
    • A general danger sign:
      • Inability to breastfeed or drink
      • Convulsion
      • Lethargy or reduced level of consciousness
